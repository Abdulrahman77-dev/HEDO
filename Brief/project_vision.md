# Vision Statement

AI Accessibility Production Platform (AAPP) exists to transform how enterprise organizations produce accessible digital documents.

Instead of forcing organizations to build workflows around multiple disconnected software products, AAPP creates a unified ecosystem where every stage of accessibility production is performed within one intelligent platform.

Our goal is not to compete with existing PDF remediation tools.

Our goal is to replace the entire accessibility production workflow.

# Industry Problems

The accessibility industry suffers from several major problems.

## Workflow Fragmentation

Users constantly switch between multiple applications.

Every software introduces another learning curve.

Every additional tool increases production costs.

---

## OCR Quality

Many scanned documents produce poor OCR.

Existing workflows require external OCR software.

Multiple OCR passes often reduce efficiency.

Document quality varies dramatically.

---

## File Size Explosion

Large PDF files often require splitting before tagging.

Cloud tagging engines perform better on smaller documents.

After merging:

Output file size can become hundreds of times larger.

This creates storage problems, sharing problems and performance issues.

Current workaround depends on legacy Adobe Acrobat versions that are approaching end-of-support.

---

## Manual Accessibility Work

Accessibility specialists still perform large amounts of repetitive work:

Reading Order

Lists

Tables

Forms

Alt Text

Headers

Validation

Quality Assurance

Many tasks should be automated.

---

## Enterprise Scale

Government agencies and universities often manage:

Thousands of PDFs

Hundreds of thousands of pages

Millions of document elements

Current workflows do not scale efficiently.
